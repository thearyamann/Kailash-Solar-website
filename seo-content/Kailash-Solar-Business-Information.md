# Kailash Solar — Core Business Information & SEO Guidelines

## Local Roots
Kailash Solar is proudly from Meerut and serves Meerut homeowners and surrounding areas.
Advantage: Faster communication, local site visits, easier coordination, local accountability, understanding of local electricity/DISCOM processes, continued after-sales relationship.

## Free Home Visit & Solar Survey
- No obligation. Just clear numbers before deciding.
- Process: Check rooftop, understand electricity consumption, assess roof conditions, recommend system, discuss expected generation & savings, explain government assistance and installation process.

## Free Solar Bill Check
- Customers can send their latest electricity bill via WhatsApp.
- Helps estimate: recommended solar size, expected generation, savings, and government assistance.

## Product Quality
- **Adani N-Type TOPCon Panels:** High efficiency, bifacial technology, up to 30-year performance warranty.
- **Solar Structure:** Hot-Dipped Galvanized (HDG), pre-cut, pre-drilled, no cutting or welding on the roof.

## Installation & Support
- **100% In-House Installation:** Trained Kailash employees, no subcontractors. Better accountability and control.
- **Net Metering Support:** Complete assistance with application, DISCOM coordination, and grid connection.
- **After-Sales Support:** 365-day service support for troubleshooting, maintenance, and warranty coordination.

## Subsidy & Financing
- **PM Surya Ghar Subsidy:** Up to ₹1,08,000 in combined assistance for eligible residential customers. Fast, end-to-end documentation support.
- **Financing:** Low-EMI loan options available starting from competitive rates (e.g. 5.75%, subject to lender approval).

- Material delivery in 2-3 days (subject to availability).
