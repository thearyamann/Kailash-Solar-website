## 3. Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
Kailash Solar differentiates itself through a premium, end-to-end service model (similar to top national players like Solar Square), focusing on:
- **Hassle-Free Subsidy Claim:** Complete handholding for PM Surya Ghar (up to ₹78,000 Central) + UPNEDA (up to ₹30,000 State) subsidies.
- **Premium Tier-1 Components:** Exclusive use of BIS-certified, Tier-1 modules (Adani, Waaree) and high-efficiency inverters.
- **Zero Cost EMI / Financing:** Assistance with collateral-free solar loans from nationalized banks to make solar adoption financially seamless.
- **End-to-End Execution:** From 3D site survey and shadow analysis to net-metering approval and 5-year AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract). No subcontracting.
- **Transparent Pricing:** No hidden costs; accurate ROI and payback period calculations provided upfront.

## 4. Service Offerings
### A. Residential Rooftop Solar (B2C)
Targeted at homeowners looking to eliminate electricity bills and claim government subsidies.
- On-Grid and Hybrid Systems (1kW to 10kW+).
- Complete PM Surya Ghar portal registration and PVVNL net-metering handling.

### B. Commercial & Industrial Solar EPC (B2B)
Targeted at factories, schools, hospitals, and commercial complexes looking for tax depreciation and OPEX reduction.
- Large scale CAPEX and OPEX models.
- Custom structural engineering for complex rooftops.
